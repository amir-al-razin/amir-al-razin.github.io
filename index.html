Tariq (طريق)

Interactive Quranic Arabic immersion platform based on the Esho Arbi Shikhi curriculum by Maulana Abu Taher Misbah. Employs the Direct Method to teach comprehension without relying on constant translation.

Sequential Harakat Fading: Gradually hides vowel diacritics as learners advance, transitioning them to reading unvoweled Arabic text.
AI & Speech Feedback: RAG-assisted explanation helper and speech-to-text pronunciation evaluation.
Kotlin (Android) Java / Spring Boot Python (RAG / STT) JQuranTree
